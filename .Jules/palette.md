## 2026-04-22 - [Custom Accessible Confirmation Dialogs]
**Learning:** Replacing native `window.confirm` with a custom component allows for better visual consistency and accessibility. Using `role="alertdialog"` and proper ARIA labels ensures that screen readers can correctly identify the intent of the dialog.
**Action:** Always prefer custom accessible modals over native browser dialogs for destructive actions to maintain UX delight and accessibility standards.
