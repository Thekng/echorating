## 2025-05-18 - MetricsSearch Accessibility and Keyboard Usability
**Learning:** In search dropdown components with dynamic query filtering, adding explicit `aria-label` attributes, `type="button"`, `role="status"`, `aria-live="polite"`, and `focus-visible` ring styling ensures full screen reader and keyboard accessibility without introducing extra dependencies or complex state.
**Action:** Always verify search inputs and icon buttons include clear ARIA labels, `aria-hidden` decorative icons, and live status announcements for empty states.
