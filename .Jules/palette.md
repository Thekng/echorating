## 2025-05-15 - Accessible ConfirmDialog Patterns
**Learning:** To maintain a micro-UX component like 'ConfirmDialog' under the 50-line limit while ensuring accessibility, prioritizing 'autoFocus' on the primary button and global 'Escape' key listeners provides a robust baseline for keyboard users without needing complex focus-trapping logic.
**Action:** Use 'autoFocus' and simple 'useEffect' event listeners for common accessible modal behaviors in small-scoped components.
