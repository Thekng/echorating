## 2026-08-27 - Search Inputs with Popup Lists Require Combobox & Keydown Listeners
**Learning:** Adding `role="combobox"`, `aria-expanded`, `aria-controls`, and `role="listbox"` to search inputs with dropdown results improves screen reader context, and handling `Escape` key events ensures intuitive keyboard dismissability.
**Action:** When implementing or refining search components with floating result panels, ensure standard WAI-ARIA combobox patterns and `Escape` key dismissal are added.
