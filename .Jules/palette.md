## 2026-09-25 - Unique IDs and ARIA Radio Groups in Dynamic Form Metrics
**Learning:** In dynamically generated form controls (such as `DailyLogForm`), metric input controls must receive unique `id` attributes linked to label `htmlFor` props. For radio button selection modes, standard `htmlFor` on the outer label is invalid because a non-interactive wrapper `div` cannot be linked; instead, applying `role="radiogroup"` with `aria-labelledby={`metric-label-${metric.id}`}` on the wrapper and linking inner radio inputs to individual option labels ensures proper screen reader accessibility.
**Action:** Always generate unique prefixed IDs for dynamic form fields and use `role="radiogroup"` with `aria-labelledby` for radio groups rather than linking `htmlFor` to a wrapper element.
